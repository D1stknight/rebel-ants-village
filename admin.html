Performance

Every Universe page that is in the rooms sends a short report once a minute while it is played: its frames a second, its slow frames (over 50 ms) and freezes (over a second), its ping to the rooms and dropped connections. Numbers only, no names. Under 30 fps feels choppy; a ping of 200 ms or more feels laggy.

This site

What players see on the landing page and the Badge Wall

Content

Announcements

The line scrolling along the bottom of the landing page (where players connect their wallet). One announcement per line, up to 12; each shows after a ✦. Saved here, it shows to everyone within a minute.

Badges on the Badge Wall

Universe's badges show on the Rebel Economy's Badge Wall with every other app's, and on the Rebel Console's Badges page. A signed-in player's badges go there when their story saves. Send everyone's sends the badges already in every saved story now, for players who haven't played since (each badge goes once). Chest and game badges wait until Universe's own points record has those chests and first clears.

The 3D engine and its weekly robot

Babylon updates

The site keeps its own copy of Babylon, so it never changes by itself. Babylon puts out a new version most weeks and you don't need each one. Every Tuesday (five days after Babylon's weekly release, so its quick fixes are in) a robot tests the newest on all 15 villages, the Rebel Forge, the NPC Forge and the terrain lab, and says here whether it is safe.
