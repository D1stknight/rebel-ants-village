# Forge Rigger worker

Runs the Forge Rigger (Blender `bpy` 5.2, Python 3.13) inside a Vercel Sandbox so a Forge build becomes a playable village Rebel without manual steps.

- `setup.sh` builds the worker once (system libs, Python 3.13 venv with bpy/numpy/scipy/pillow, this code, master ant clips, the mocap pack). The API snapshots that sandbox; every rig job starts from the snapshot.
- `job.sh <glb_url> <name> <job_dir>` runs the full pipeline: normalize → landmarks → skeleton → weights → bind + fixarm → handfix → clothbones → master clips → CMU/Mixamo moves (`anim/clips.json`) → fixarm → footfix → export (decimate 0.35, 1536/512 textures) → `qa_job.py`.
- Mocap sources (`pack/`) are not in this repo. The admin route uploads them to Blob and passes their URLs to `setup.sh`.
- After changing anything here, push, then rebuild the worker from the Forge admin (it pins the commit).

API: `/api/forge-rig-worker` (admin: pack upload, setup, status), `/api/forge-rig-start`, `/api/forge-rig-status`.

## NPC villagers (NPC Forge, `FORGE_NPC=1`, Oct 7)
- The admin NPC Forge (npc-forge.html) forges an NPC like a Rebel: Miguel's four renders (front / back / left / right) -> Meshy multi-image-to-3D -> this rigger with `npc: true` on /api/forge-rig-start, which runs job.sh with `FORGE_NPC=1`.
- NPC mode: moves from `anim/clips_npc.json` (Miguel's Mixamo NPC pack: talking, talking2, waving, wave_short, bow, pointing, pick_up, nod_yes, shake_no, clapping, rallying, yelling, look_around, idle_looking; `.npz` in `pack/mixamo/`, uploaded from the NPC Forge, kept off GitHub) instead of the fight and weapon moves; no weapons / moves pack; headfix skips the gestures (it cut nods to a tenth and flipped the head in pick_up); `handfloor.py` keeps fingertips on the floor when a long-armed villager reaches down; 1024 base textures.
- QA (`qa_job.py`, NPC mode): stretch on the NPC clips plus torn pieces (edges stretched > 2.5x and longer than 15 % / 30 % of the height), glove vertices not driven by the hand (leftover parts), mesh lean vs rest in idle / talking / walk (6 / 6 / 9 deg), sinking into the floor (> 3 cm), head snaps (> 30 deg in one frame). The verdict and per-clip numbers show in the NPC Forge.
- The NPC gesture pack must be in the worker image (upload in the NPC Forge, then Rebuild worker); an NPC job without it stops at "moves" naming the missing file.
- The earlier local NPC route (npcjob.sh + low-poly generator sources) is retired: those sources broke under the rigger (fused props, elbows on the armour, crooked spines).

## Games pack (v2.24, Oct 16)
- Miguel's 14 Mixamo moves for the village games (fishing cast + idle, digging, planting, throw, goalie throw, the axe combo, cheering, pushing x2, four dances) as `pack/mixamo/gm_*.npz`, uploaded in the NPC Forge's Rig worker box (same Upload button) and built into the worker by Rebuild worker.
- `anim/clips_games.json` cuts them into 16 clips (game_cast / game_reel / game_fish_wait, game_dig, game_plant, game_pick_up, game_throw, game_throw_far, game_chop, game_cheer, game_push, game_push_walk, dance_wave / tut / house / gangnam). job.sh retargets them after the weapon moves, they go through the same cleanups, keep their own head motion (FORGE_HEAD_SKIP) and their hand poses (handswap: grip on rod / shovel / axe, open palms to push and dance), and ship in `games.glb` (skeleton + those clips; rig.glb and moves.glb are unchanged: export.py drops FORGE_GAME_CLIPS too). A worker without the pack makes no games.glb.
- v2.30 Miguel's second set (Oct 17): rope pull, the downward melee strike (split into game_hammer_up / game_hammer: raise, then strike on the beat), center block, the sword-and-shield block, idle and two hit reactions, the goalkeeper catch played backwards (`"reverse": true` in retarget_bvh.py) as a jump shot, the dribble, the bowling throw (keeps its step forward: `"in_place": false`) and swimming (`"grounded": false`): 12 more clips from 11 more gm_*.npz. A worker image without some of the files makes the pack without their moves (job.sh logs each missing file); result.json lists the pack's clips (gamesClips) and the NPC Forge remakes packs that lack the current ones. The Pro Longbow pack is already in the weapon moves (bow_*), the second Goalie Throw is the first one's file.
- v2.31 (Oct 17): Miguel's sneak walk (game_sneak, one 0.87 s loop from gm_sneak_walk.npz) for Silent Steps: the Rebel walks with it while that game runs. Loops loop: a clip with `"loop": true` (the four dances, game_sneak, game_plant, game_push_walk) has each bone's turn from its last pose to its first (and the hips' move, and the floor shift) spread over the clip in retarget_bvh.py, so it runs back into its start without a jump (the 1.6 s dance cuts jumped 13-23 cm / up to 58 degrees at the seam, every loop); antennafix runs its spring twice on the games pack's loops so the stalks sway into the loop the way they sway out. The dances are longer cuts with more moves before they repeat (wave 5.5 s, tut 5.3, house 4.4, gangnam 3.3), for the Rebels' games pack and the NPCs' party dances alike.
- forge-rig-status stores a new rig's pack at `forge:games:v1:<rig url>`; /api/forge-rig-moves (the moves re-run for an existing rig) makes it too; headcloth copies it to the cloth rig; /api/forge-weapon?action=moves answers `{ moves, games }`. The Rig worker box lists the Rebels in play (/api/forge-games-packs) and makes the missing packs, two at a time.
