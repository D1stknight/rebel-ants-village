The colony's field guide

How Rebel Universe works

A 3D world of the Rebel Ants: fifteen villages, the story of the colony, over 150 games and puzzles, live shows, and other players walking around with you. Here is everything in one page.

1

Pick a character

Your own Rebel, Chumpz or Saint from your wallet, or any Free card. No NFT needed.

2

Sign in

Discord, or a name and PIN. Your points, badges and story follow you to any device.

3

Enter Village

You start in the Hub. Walk up to anything glowing and press O.

01

Your character

The cards in the lobby are who you can play. Pick one, then press Enter Village.

Your own NFTs

Connect your wallet and your Rebel Ants (Battle for the Colony), Chumpz (ApeChain) and Saints (Ethereum) show up as cards. A card marked Ready has its 3D character; the others play as a stand-in until you forge them.

Free cards

No NFT? The cards marked Free are forged characters anyone can play. Guests who pick nothing start as Rebel #262.

Rebel #001

The colony's first Rebel. In Universe he is Ant-Thony, your guide and the colony's comedian, so you will meet him around the villages.

Change any time

Go back to the lobby (the round Rebel button, then Back to Lobby) and pick another card. Your story and points stay with your account, not with one character.

02

Your wallet

Connect Wallet only reads which NFTs you hold. Playing never asks you to send anything.

Browser wallet

MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Rabby, Brave Wallet or any other extension on a computer.

WalletConnect

On a phone, in Safari, or without an extension: scan the code or open your wallet app.

Signing a message with your wallet is free. It proves the wallet is yours, so your best scores can go on the games' top 10 boards under your Rebel's name.

03

Your account

Sign in with the round button at the top left of the lobby, or from the Journal in Universe.

Discord

One tap if you are in the Rebel Ants Discord.

A name and PIN

No Discord? Pick a name and a 4 to 6 digit PIN. It is the same account as Rebel Ants After Dark and the Playground, with the same points.

What it saves

Your Rebel Points, your badges, the chests you opened, the games you cleared and your place in the story. Sign in on another device and carry on where you left off.

Playing as a guest

Guests can walk, play and read the chat. Their progress stays on that device and their points are practice points. Sign in later and the story you played joins your account.

04

Rebel Points

Rebel Points are the colony's loyalty points. You earn them by playing, once per account for each find, and spend them in the Forge.

102treasure chests, from wooden boxes to legend chests
150+games and puzzles: the first clear of each pays
8place riddles hidden around the villages
300badges in your Journal

Earning

Open a chest, clear a game for the first time, solve a place riddle. Each pays your account once. A few finds you can repeat, like the Treasure Dig on the Chumpz beach, count toward a cap of 2,000 points a day.

Getting more

Signed in, open your account in the lobby and press Get Rebel Points: the Rebel Economy shop sells point packs by card, APE or ETH.

05

The Forge

The Forge turns your NFT into a 3D character that walks, runs, fights, dances and sits in Universe.

How a forge goes

Pick your card in the lobby and press Forge. The Forge paints your character from your NFT's picture, sculpts it in 3D, then rigs it with all its moves. Then Awaken it and press Make This My Rebel.

What it costs

Each forge costs 20,000 Rebel Points from the account you are signed in with. You need to hold the NFT you forge.

Gloves

On an awakened Rebel or Saint you can fit the gloves: size, cuff and colours, with a live preview, free a few times a day.

Chumpz and Saints

Holders forge their Chumpz and Saints the same way and play them in every village.

06

The Universe

You start in the Hub. Every village gate takes you to the others.

Fifteen villages

The Hub, the eleven faction villages (Ronin, Samurai, Shogun, Bushi, Buke, Ashigaru, Kenshi, Yamabushi, Sohei, Wokou, Warrior) and the friend villages: Cute & Creepy, Chumpz Cove and Saints of LA. Walk up to a gate and press O to travel.

The story

The Hub Journey teaches you the ropes, then Book One runs fifty chapters across the villages. Thirty side quests, and the friend villages' own tales: Moon Tales, Chumpz Cove and Saints of LA.

Your mission

The gold ◆ circle under your Rebel button shows what to do next, and a gold diamond on the map points the way. It opens by itself when the mission changes.

Games, rooms, secrets

About ten games in every village, rooms behind many doors, a hundred secrets and small ants that whisper hints. Your Journal keeps count of all of it.

07

Ant-Thony

Rebel #001, the colony's comedian and your guide. Ask him anything about Universe.

Where he is

He walks the villages on a timetable, ten minutes in each, back in the Hub every third stop. When he is in your village he is the gold dot on your map, and your Rebel panel always says where he is.

Talking to him

Walk up and he says hello. Press O (or tap him) to open his chat and type a question: a game, a quest, a place, being stuck. He answers out loud. Signed-in players sometimes get a little tip.

His house

From 2 to 7 in the morning, New York time, he sleeps in his little house by the Hub's pond. Knock and wake him if you must. The rest of the day the note over his bed says where he went.

His club

Antthony's Comedy Club is in the forest west of the Hub. Find his Wall of Fame, the snack bar and the Roast Champion trophy, and catch his shows on stage.

08

Playing together

Everyone in your village is live around you. Your missions, chests and scores stay your own.

Chat

Press Enter (the chat button on a phone). Nearby, the whole village, or a whisper. Signed-in players write; guests read.

Voice

Hold X (or Talk on a phone) and the players near you hear you. Signed-in players only. Allow the microphone when the browser asks.

Emotes and groups

Press E to cheer or dance. Click a name to invite them to your group: up to eight, with their own chat and voice across villages.

Staying safe

Click any name to mute, block or report. Moderators read the reports and can mute or remove players.

09

Live events

Shows and meetups happen on the Colony Stage. When one is on, LIVE NOW shows at the top of the lobby.

Joining

Press Join on the LIVE NOW banner and you go straight there. Already in Universe? The LIVE banner's Go there button takes you, and a gold mark shows the door.

Golden tickets

The front row at the Comedy Club is for golden ticket holders: win one in the free raffle before the doors open, or buy one with Rebel Points.

Seats

Walk up behind a chair and press O to sit. Then 1 claps and 2 laughs; everyone sees it. O again to stand.

Speaking at an event

Raise your hand from the stage bar. A host can bring you up to speak to the whole room.

11

Photos and clips

Press P for a photo, or Shift + P to record a clip of up to a minute (Photo and Clip under ••• on a phone). Share them as a polaroid to X, Instagram, TikTok or Discord, or save them. A clip you close without saving is gone.

12

Questions

Do I need an NFT to play?

No. Pick a Free card, or just press Enter Village as a guest. NFTs let you play your own character and forge it.

Does it cost anything?

Playing is free. Forging a character costs Rebel Points, which you earn by playing or can buy in the Rebel Economy shop.

Where is my progress saved?

Signed in, on your account, so it follows you to any device. As a guest, on this device only: sign in to keep it.

I can't chat or talk.

Chat and voice need you to be signed in. For voice, hold X (or Talk), allow the microphone, and on an iPhone tap the screen once so sound can start. The other player has to be near you, in the same place.

My phone reloads or shows a white screen.

That is the phone running out of memory. Close other tabs and apps and try again; after a crash Universe runs lighter on that phone for a few days.

I'm stuck in a quest.

Open the ◆ circle to see your next step, follow the gold diamond on the map, or find Ant-Thony and ask him.
